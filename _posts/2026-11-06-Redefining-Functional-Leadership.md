title: "Stop Assisting Your Department and Start Redesigning It"
excerpt: "Most functional leaders I talk to describe their AI plan as giving their people better tools. Procurement gets a contract summarizer, HR gets a drafting assistant, and finance gets faster reconciliation. Each of those helps, and none of them changes what the function is. A department built on the assumption that reading, drafting, matching, and routing are expensive human tasks does not become a different department because those tasks got cheaper."
